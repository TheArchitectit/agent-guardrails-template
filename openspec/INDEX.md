## Reading the status

- **CLOSED** — the spec explicitly says CLOSED or every requirement row is
  `EXERCISED`.
- **PARTIAL** — at least one requirement is `EXERCISED` and at least one is
  `WIRED`, `ABSENT`, `NOT_RUN`, or `DIFFERS`.
- **OPEN** — a closure table exists, but no requirement reaches `EXERCISED`.
- **UNKNOWN** — no closure table and no explicit `Status:` line exists. This is
  a documentation gap, not a claim that the work is unfinished.
