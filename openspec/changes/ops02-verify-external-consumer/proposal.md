# Proposal

## Why

Producer and verifier unit tests do not demonstrate that a real enterprise consumer accepts the emitted evidence or applies the intended identity and policy contract. The actual consumer and whether external Gate 4 applies are not selected in this checkout.

## What Changes

- Identify the real consumer and applicable acceptance boundary; capture version-bound integration results for signed evidence verification, identity binding, and policy enforcement. Preserve the rule that receipts and observe-only checkers do not authorize effects.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `external-consumer-verification`: verify the external consumer contract evidence for an enterprise rollout.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Consumer integration adapter/runbook and a redacted evidence reference. Consumer credentials, payloads, and personal data remain in the approved private test system.
