# numbered-design-status-evidence Specification

## Purpose

Numbered design documents retain dated context while making current evidence and residual requirements reviewable.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Reconciled claims retain dates and evidence
A reconciled numbered design status claim SHALL retain its historical date, cite the inspected source or named test evidence, and state any requirement or verification scope that remains unresolved.

#### Scenario: Dated claim retains evidence and residuals
- **WHEN** a numbered design status claim is reconciled
- **THEN** its historical date and supporting source or test references remain visible and unresolved requirements or verification limits are stated.
